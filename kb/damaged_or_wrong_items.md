# Damaged or Wrong Items

## Report a problem
Contact support within 7 calendar days after delivery if an item arrived damaged, broken, or different from the order. If you received the wrong item, include that detail in the report.

## Helpful details
Include the order number, a description of the issue, and clear photos when available. Do not send sensitive payment details.

## Human review
A support reviewer checks the report and available order details before deciding what options may apply.

## Keep the item
Keep the item and packaging until support advises on next steps. Do not assume a replacement or refund is approved before review.
