# Order Cancellation

## Request timing
Customers may ask to cancel within 2 hours of placing an order. A human reviewer checks whether processing has already started.

## Before dispatch
An order that has not entered dispatch may be eligible for cancellation. A reviewer confirms the order status and decision.

## After dispatch
An order already handed to the carrier cannot be stopped through this cancellation process. The return policy may apply after delivery.

## How to ask
Contact support with the order number as soon as possible. A request is not a cancellation confirmation.
