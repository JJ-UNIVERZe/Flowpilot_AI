# Payment Methods

## Accepted methods
The demo checkout accepts major credit and debit cards, a digital wallet, and bank transfer. A reviewer can confirm which methods are enabled for a specific order.

## Currency
Prices and charges are shown in the checkout currency before an order is submitted. A reviewer should resolve any difference between the displayed amount and a payment record.

## Payment status
A pending payment may take up to 2 business days to update. Contact support with the order number if the status does not change.

## Payment questions
Support can review a charge or declined payment. The customer should not send full card or bank account numbers.
