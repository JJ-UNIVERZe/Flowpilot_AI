# Refund Timelines

## Review first
Refund requests are reviewed by a person. Timing estimates begin only after a refund is approved.

## Card refunds
After approval, a card refund usually takes 5-7 business days to arrive. The card provider may take longer to post it.

## Bank refunds
After approval, a bank refund usually takes 5-10 business days to arrive. Banks may use different processing times.

## Checking status
Contact support with the order number if the estimated period has passed. A reviewer can check the recorded refund status.
