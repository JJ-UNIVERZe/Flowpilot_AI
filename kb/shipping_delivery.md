# Shipping and Delivery

## Dispatch estimate
An in-stock order is generally prepared for dispatch within 1-2 business days. A reviewer can confirm the status of a specific order.

## Delivery estimate
Standard shipping is estimated to take 3-5 business days after dispatch. The estimate can vary by destination and carrier.

## Tracking
Tracking details may take up to 24 hours after dispatch to show movement. Contact support if tracking remains unchanged.

## Delays
Address issues or carrier delays can affect estimates. A support reviewer checks the order before advising on next steps.
